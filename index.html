00 / THE PROBLEM

Two broken defaults we are replacing

COGNITIVE DEPENDENCY

Standard AI creates the illusion of learning.

ChatGPT and Claude are optimized to be maximally helpful and solve problems fast. A student pastes a hard question, reads a clean explanation, feels confident, and then fails a closed-book exam because their own brain never did the retrieval work.

Frontier labs benchmark whether models pass exams. Almost nobody benchmarks which AI loop actually raises a human's unaided score 48 hours later.

GATEKEPT OPPORTUNITY

Admissions truth is locked behind pricey agencies.

Outside wealthy schools, ambitious teenagers pay thousands of dollars to prep centers and consulting agencies just for basic exam prep, realistic university outcome data, and scholarship checklists that should be public knowledge.

We pair real graduate outcome data and verified student reviews with open full-ride scholarship blueprints so talent is never capped by family income.

Four active tracks under one public lab

An open experimental harness running randomized A/B studies across real students to measure 48-hour unaided transfer rates (direct answers vs. diagnostic Socratic forcing vs. mutated spaced retrieval).

03 / OPEN COLLABORATION

Anyone can contribute today

Unaided Lab is built as an open collective, not a closed company. Students, teachers, researchers, and engineers from any country can open a pull request.

01
