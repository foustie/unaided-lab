Emergent Ventures

Win the battle, lose the war.

Everyone in AI is focused on scaling compute and making models smarter, while ignoring what happens to human brains. Frontier models are trained to hand over the finished answer on turn one. A student pastes a hard problem into a chat box, feels like they understood it, and then freezes the second the test or real work is closed-book.

Big labs benchmark whether models can pass human exams (MMLU, MATH, GPQA). Almost nobody runs open experiments measuring whether an AI interaction actually raises a human's unaided score 48 hours later.

At the same time, real exam prep and transparent paths into top universities or full-ride scholarships stay locked behind expensive courses and consulting agencies. Outside wealthy schools, smart kids pay for information and training that should be open and free.

What we are building

Anti-dependency learning loops

Free tools that refuse instant crutches, pinpoint the exact missing step in a student's thinking, and resurface mutated problems two days later with zero AI assistance.

48-hour unaided transfer benchmark (U48)

Open randomized A/B studies across real students measuring cold closed-book retention across different AI teaching loops, published with reproducible prompts and aggregate data.

Open global student blueprints

Verified university outcomes, real graduate employment data, and 100% funded international scholarship roadmaps so students anywhere can see their real options without predatory agencies.

Starting from real open-source proof

Built on ENTprep (open source), a free exam trainer live at entprep.org that reached 7,630+ students on $0 of ads and earned an Emergent Ventures grant.
