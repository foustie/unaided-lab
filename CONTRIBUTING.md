# Contributing to Unaided Lab

Unaided Lab is an open collective. You do not need a degree or permission to contribute.

## Four ways to help right now

1. **Add a full-ride scholarship:** Open a PR adding verified 2027 eligibility rules, coverage details, required exams, and official links to `/datasets/scholarships-2027.json`. Every entry must link to an official government or university source.
2. **Add national exam or cutoff data:** Contribute structured JSON for university grant cutoffs, graduate employment stats, or official national exam blueprints in your country.
3. **Build or improve learning tools:** Help expand the anti-dependency study loop, localization, or offline/low-bandwidth PWA support.
4. **Contribute uplift evaluations:** Add mutated problem generators, prompt guardrails that resist "just give me the answer" jailbreaks, or evaluation scripts.

## Ground rules

- **Verify every fact:** Admissions deadlines and cutoff scores affect real teenagers' futures. Link official sources in every dataset PR.
- **No paywalls on core public goods:** Core datasets, blueprints, and open research stay free forever.
