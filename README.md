# Unaided Lab

**AI that makes you smarter without it.**

Unaided Lab is an open public-good research lab building free anti-dependency AI learning systems, transparent global university and scholarship blueprints, and open benchmarks measuring real unaided human learning gains.

## Why this exists

1. **AI cognitive dependency is a global crisis.** Frontier models are trained to give instant answers. Students read smooth solutions, feel like they understand, and then fail closed-book exams or real problem solving once the prompt box is gone.
2. **Opportunity is gatekept by agencies.** Outside wealthy schools, teenagers pay predatory fees to prep centers and study-abroad consultants for information and practice tools that should be open and free.

## Active Tracks

| Track | Status | Summary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01. ENTprep** | Live testbed | Free bilingual (RU/KZ) exam trainer built solo at 17; reached 7,630+ students on $0 ads, backed by Emergent Ventures ($10K). |
| **02. Global Student Blueprint** | Building now | Verified side-by-side roadmaps comparing local grants with 100% funded international scholarships plus real graduate outcomes. |
| **03. Human Uplift Benchmark** | Research | Open A/B harness measuring whether AI tutoring methods actually raise unaided human scores 48 hours later on mutated closed-book problems. |
| **04. Open Datasets** | Open JSON | Community-maintained registries in `/datasets` covering scholarships, university cutoffs, and graduate outcomes. |
